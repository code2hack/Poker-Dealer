# Settle the 480×640 Poker reading interaction

Type: prototype
Status: open

## Question

On the real 480×640 RG-glasses HUD, what typography, safe area, card chrome, scrolling unit, live-update indicator, conclusion/full toggle, and deck navigation make 20,000-character cards readable without semantic truncation or forced scrolling?

The answer must come from a minimal on-device prototype and live human reaction, and must preserve the card/viewport semantics already fixed in `SPEC.md`.
